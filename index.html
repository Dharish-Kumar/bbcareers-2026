Our Journey

Five founders. Few rented warehouses. One bold idea to transform how India shops.

bigbasket was founded by V.S. Sudhakar, Hari Menon, V.S. Ramesh, Vipul Parekh, and Abhinay Choudhari. What began with less than $1 million in seed capital grew into a business with annual gross sales surpassing $1 billion within just 15 years.

Today, bigbasket is a Tata Enterprise. As one of India’s leading online supermarkets and quick-commerce platforms, we deliver everything from farm-fresh produce to the latest smartphones. Built on the power of "now," we serve millions of customers while redefining convenience at scale.

Our Diversity & Inclusion Initiative

Our journey toward building a truly diverse and equitable workforce took a major leap forward in 2019 with Aarambh. We believe a happy workplace is one where people feel heard, respected, and empowered. By embracing our differences, we bring together diverse perspectives that help us learn, grow, and innovate.
